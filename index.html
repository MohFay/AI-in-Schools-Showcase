Start with the big picture

Powerful tools need thoughtful users.

The integration of AI into learning gives students access to resources, efficient tools, and tailored experiences. But usefulness does not remove responsibility. AI can help explain a concept, suggest an approach, or speed up a first draft. It can also be wrong, biased, or so convenient that it gets in the way of learning.

This guide explores how AI is being used across career fields — and what students need to know to use it wisely.

Use AI to learn. Ask for explanations, examples, and hints — not just final answers.

Check the output. Verify claims, calculations, sources, and code before relying on them.

Protect people. Follow class rules, respect privacy and credit, and be honest about AI use.
